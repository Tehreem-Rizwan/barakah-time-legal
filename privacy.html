Barakah Time

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Barakah Time app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what choices you have. Barakah Time is developed by Tehreem Rizwan, an individual developer in Pakistan.

In short: the app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We never sell or rent your data. You can use most of the app without an account, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

2. How location is used

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your device. Your GPS location is kept on your device and is not stored on our servers. If you are signed in and set your location manually, that city and its coordinates are saved with your settings in your account so they sync to your other devices. To show the name of your city, your coordinates are sent to your device's geocoding service, or to the public OpenStreetMap Nominatim service if that is unavailable; the last city name found is kept on your device. When you open the mosque finder, your coordinates are sent to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service to look up nearby mosques. When you type a city name, your device's geocoding service may be used to convert it to coordinates. You can instead enter your location manually and deny location access.

3. Esaal-e-Sawab pages are shared

An Esaal-e-Sawab page can be opened by anyone who is signed in and has its 8-character code. Everyone with the code can see the page details and the names that contributors use, together with their contributions. Pages cannot be searched or listed. Only share the code with people you trust. The person who created a page can delete it at any time.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and store your account data. Firebase privacy
  • Supabase to store profile photos. Profile photos are stored at a public web address so the app can display them. Supabase privacy
  • Google Sign-In if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Al Quran Cloud (alquran.cloud) for Quran text, translations and recitation audio. Recitations you listen to are saved on your device so they play offline; the oldest are removed automatically.
  • Google Fonts to download the app's fonts, including the Quran font you choose. Google Fonts privacy
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API for nearby mosques, Nominatim to show your city name). OpenStreetMap privacy

These services receive only what they need to work (for example your IP address when your device connects to them) and handle it under their own privacy policies.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

6. Notifications

Prayer, sehri/iftar and remembrance-day reminders, and the optional night-recitation and Surah Al-Kahf reminders, are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app settings or in your phone's settings.

7. Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your synced data, your profile photo and any Esaal-e-Sawab pages you created. Pages created by other people stay, but you can remove them from your list.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account at its.tehreem2830@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Data on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your account data is restricted to your own signed-in account by security rules. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
